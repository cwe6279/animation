`eye_left.png` and `eye_right.png` are an original macro photograph of a cat's eye by the
repository author, released under the same licence as the rest of this repository. The
polar iris strip in `eye_unwrapped/` is derived from those two images by tools/unwrap_eye.py.

Live eye parts in `eye/` are the `dragonEye` design from Adafruit's Uncanny Eyes project
(https://github.com/adafruit/uncanny_eyes, MIT) with the iris hue shifted to green. The cat
currently renders from `eye/`; `eye_unwrapped/` is kept as the conversion of the original
photograph.

Sound effects in `sounds/`, when present, come from Pixabay (https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/),
which is free to use without attribution.
